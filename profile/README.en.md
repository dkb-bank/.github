# Open Source. Building Together.

Digital solutions are created together. Open-source projects make knowledge and software accessible and provide foundations that many people can build on. That is why we support open-source projects with donations.

## Why We Support Open Source

Behind great open-source software are people and communities who share ideas and develop solutions together. Our donations support the work of these people and communities.
